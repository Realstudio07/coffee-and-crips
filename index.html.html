Our Space

Built like a greenhouse, run like a coffee bar.

Inside, a lattice of white beams holds up a curtain of trailing vines and warm filament bulbs — the same structure you'll spot the moment you walk in. It's an industrial shell softened with a lot of green.

The counter runs the length of the room, pulling espresso shots while the kitchen turns out momos, fried rice and fusion plates behind it. Pull a stool at the bar or take a table by the glass front — either way, the light stays golden till close.

When the Hyderabad evening cools off, the patio out back takes over: string lights, banana leaves overhead, and tables that stay full past sunset.
